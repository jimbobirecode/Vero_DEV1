Outlets

Everything about your outlets lives here — the name, the minimum spend that qualifies for a survey, how many days before a member can be re-surveyed, and which questions they are asked. Leave the questions on "Default for visit type" for ordinary dining; choose a template when an outlet needs its own, such as a racquets centre or a lesson. Changes save automatically.

Add outlet

Servers

Front-line servers and waitstaff credited with sales — this is the list the Server dropdown offers when you log a visit, and who receives a shift survey. Separate from Team members above: a General Manager does not belong here unless they actually work the floor. A phone number or email is what makes someone reachable — anyone without either is credited for their sales but cannot be sent a shift survey. Edit a phone or email directly in the list; changes save as you leave the field.
Add a server under Team members → Add a person, choosing Servers — one place for everyone, whichever list they belong on.

Survey delivery

Surveys are queued when shift data is uploaded and go out once a day at the time you choose here. Changing the time takes effect straight away — if the new time has already passed today, that day's batch goes out within a minute. Nothing is ever sent between 8 PM and 8 AM Eastern.
Eastern Time

How often one member can be surveyed

Several cheques at the same outlet on the same day already count as one visit. This caps a member who used different outlets — the highest-spend visit is the one surveyed, and the rest are dropped rather than saved for later.

Staff shift surveys

At the end of each day, every server recorded against a visit gets a link asking how their own shift went — overall, whether they felt supported, whether the workload was manageable, and whether they had what they needed. Off by default: switching it on starts messaging your team. Nothing sends between 11 PM and 11 AM Eastern.
Eastern Time

Team members

Staff who can be assigned case alerts and manage the system. Each role sees only what it needs. Adding someone here does not put them on the Servers list below — that is who gets credited with a cheque and sent a shift survey. Somebody who does both belongs on both.

Add a person

One place for everyone. Team members hold a role and sign in to this dashboard. Servers are credited with cheques, appear in the Server dropdown when you log a visit, and receive shift surveys. Somebody who does both goes on both lists.

Upload a roster

Same "Add to" choice as above. Paste from a spreadsheet, or choose a CSV. First row must be the column names. Team members needs name (or first_name and last_name) plus optional email and role. Servers needs name plus optional phone and email. Anyone already on the list is updated rather than added twice. This does not create dashboard logins — do that below, one person at a time.

User accounts

Create dashboard logins for team members. Only Super Admins and General Managers can manage accounts.
